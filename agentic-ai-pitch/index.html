What manual work costs you today

  • Work waits in queues: tickets, approvals, reconciliations, follow-ups
  • More volume means more hiring, so margins shrink as you grow

02

How the agent closes the gap

Give it a goal, not a prompt. It works like a trained team member.

  1. Applies your business rules to the request
  2. Plans the steps and acts in your tools: CRM, ERP, email, ticketing, spreadsheets
  3. Checks its own work and retries when a step fails
  4. Hands approvals and anything unusual to a person

Good first workflows: tier-1 tickets, invoice-to-PO matching, delayed-order rerouting, new-hire setup.

04

Your questions, answered

  • What if it gets something wrong? A person approves every action at first. It earns autonomy only where results justify it.
  • Who is in control? You are: role-based permissions, approval gates for sensitive actions, a full audit log of every step.
  • Is our data safe? The agent sees only what its role allows. Your data never trains shared models.
  • Will it pay off? We agree on targets up front, baseline your numbers in week 1, and price on work completed, not messages sent.
